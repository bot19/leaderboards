**Schema:** Migrations for 1.6.5, 1.6.6, 1.6.7 and 1.6.9. They can be one file or several.

1. **Replace the `leaderboard` view.** Start from its current definition in `supabase/migrations/20261027160000_confirmation_window_minutes.sql`. Keep `security_invoker = true`, the grants, and every existing column except as listed below.

   **New and changed columns:**
   - **`ranked boolean`:** `rated_match_count > 0`. A Ladder member is **Unranked** until their first Confirmed Match. A Replay keeps `rated_match_count` correct, so a member whose only Matches are all voided becomes Unranked again.
   - **`matches integer`:** `rated_match_count`.
   - **`wins integer`:** their Confirmed Matches where their Side won. Use the same win test as `ladder_member_record`. Draws aren't wins.
   - **`rank`:** `rank()` over ranked members only, ordered `rating desc, display_name asc` as now. Null for Unranked members.

   **`rank_change`:** still previous rank minus current rank, worked out over the Ladder's latest Confirmed Match as in 1.5.0. Only members who were ranked before that Match count towards previous ranks. That means members who:
   - are ranked now,
   - joined at or before that Match was Confirmed, and
   - weren't ranked by that Match itself. Those are its Participants whose `rated_match_count` is 1.

   It's null for Unranked members, for members that Match ranked for the first time, and in the cases 1.5.0 already makes null.

2. **Database error messages.** Reword every user-facing message in a `raise exception` that says "Ladder" or "Ladder member". Use the 1.6.6 and 1.6.7 wording, and leave everything else as it is. Examples:
   - "Sign in before joining a Ladder" becomes "Sign in before joining a Leaderboard".
   - "Only a Removed Ladder member can be reinstated" becomes "Only a Removed member can be reinstated".

   Recreate each affected function from its **latest** definition across the migrations, changing only the message text. Update any test that asserts on a message.

3. Regenerate `src/types/db.ts`. `LeaderboardEntry` gains `ranked: boolean`, `wins: number` and `matches: number`, and `rank` becomes `number | null`. `listLeaderboard` orders by `rank` ascending with nulls last, then `display_name` ascending.

### 1.6.1: Joined date on the Members tab

- Add `joinedAt` to `LadderMemberRead`, read from `ladder_members.joined_at` in `listLadderMembers`.
- The second line of each row becomes `Player · Joined 12 Oct 2026`. That's the Role, the status, and then for Joined members the date.
  - Left and Removed members keep `Admin · Left` or `Player · Removed` with no date, because the database doesn't keep a leave date.
- Format the date in the User's locale as day, short month and year. Add a formatter next to `formatLocalDateTime` in `src/features/matches/labels.ts`.
- `joined_at` resets when a Left member rejoins, so the date is when they last joined. That's intended.

### 1.6.2: Long names in the member view's Match rows

`MemberMatchRow` has two lines. Fix both at 300px, with the drawer open.

- **Title line.** It reads: Pending chip if Pending, then `Won`, `Lost` or `Drew`, then `vs`, then the opponents' names. Make it ordinary inline text that fills the line and wraps onto the next line only once the line is full.
  - Drop the `inline-block max-w-[300px] truncate` span around the names. That span is what pushes the names onto their own line and lets them overflow the card.
  - A single unbroken name longer than the line breaks (`break-words`) rather than overflowing.
  - The Rating change stays on the right, top-aligned, and never wraps.
- **Details line.** One line, never wrapping, in this order:
  - **Left:** the format (`2v2`), then `with <partner>`, then the relative time.
  - **Right:** `Rating before → after`.
  - The format, the time and the Ratings always show in full. Only `with <partner>` gives way: it truncates with an ellipsis to whatever space is left. In 1v1 it's absent and nothing truncates.

### 1.6.5: Win rate on the Leaderboard

- Each Leaderboard row shows its win rate. The right-hand group reads: rank movement, then win rate, then Rating. Everyone's win rate sits directly left of their Rating, so the two line up down the list.
- Calculate it as `Math.round(100 * wins / matches)` followed by `%`. Unranked members show `–`.
- Style it `tabular-nums`, right-aligned in a fixed width so the column lines up, in the neutral grey used for the rank number. Give it an `aria-label` of `Win rate 71%`, or `No Matches yet` for Unranked members.
- At 300px, a row with a medal, a long name, `New`, `Inactive`, movement, win rate and Rating stays on one line. The name truncates first.

### 1.6.8: The bottom bar avatar opens your member view

- On `/ladders/$ladderId`, when the User has a Joined Ladder member on that Ladder, the bottom bar avatar opens the drawer's member view for that Ladder member (`nav=member&member=<their id>`).
  - Its `aria-label` is `Your record`.
  - It shows as active (`aria-current`) while that view is open.
- Everywhere else, including a Ladder page where the User isn't Joined (such as the super admin viewing someone else's Ladder), it opens the Profile view as now, labelled `Profile`.
- The route already loads the Ladder members. Pass the User's own Ladder member id into `SignedInChrome` as a prop, so the chrome doesn't fetch it again.
- The display name form stays reachable from the menu view's Profile card.

### 1.6.9: Unranked members at the bottom

- **Leaderboard tab:** Unranked members are listed after every ranked member, ordered by name. Each Unranked row shows:
  - `–` in the rank column, with no medal and no movement
  - `–` for win rate (1.6.5)
  - their Rating (1000) and the `New` badge, plus `Inactive` dimming and badge if that applies too
- **Medals and movement:** `leaderboardPodium` gets a null rank and returns nothing for it. Medals and movement apply to ranked members only.
- **Member view:**
  - An Unranked member shows `Unranked` where the rank goes.
  - A ranked member shows `#n of m`, where m is the number of ranked members.
  - Left and Removed members are unchanged.
- **Tab count:** the Rankings tab count still includes everyone on the Leaderboard.
- **Inactive:** members who have played but are Inactive stay ranked, as now.

### 1.6.10: Member view Match list follows confirmation order

- `listMemberMatchesPage` orders Pending Matches first, newest recorded first, then Confirmed Matches newest Confirmed first:
  ```ts
  .order('confirmed_at', { ascending: false, nullsFirst: true })
  .order('recorded_at', { ascending: false })
  .order('id', { ascending: false })
  ```
- Each row already shows its confirmed time once Confirmed.
- The result: the first Confirmed rows match the form dots and streak, newest first, and Rating before → after chains from one row to the next.

### Glossary

- **Ladder:** add "Shown on screen as **Leaderboard**."
- **Ladder member:** add "Shown on screen as **member**." Keep _Avoid: member (on its own)_ for code and docs.
- **Leaderboard:** "A Ladder's Joined Ladder members, ranked by Rating. Unranked members are listed after the ranked ones. Inactive members stay at their rank, marked Inactive. The Ladder page's tab for it is shown on screen as **Rankings**."
- **New term: Unranked.** "A Joined Ladder member with no Confirmed Match on the Ladder. Listed at the bottom of the Leaderboard with no rank, medal or Rank movement. Their first Confirmed Match ranks them." _Avoid: unrated, unplaced._
- **Flagged ambiguities:** add that on screen, "Leaderboard" names a Ladder and "Rankings" names its Leaderboard. In code and docs, use Ladder and Leaderboard.
