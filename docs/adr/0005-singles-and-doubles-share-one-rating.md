# Singles and doubles share one Rating per Ladder

A Ladder member has one Rating on a Ladder, and it moves for every Confirmed Match they play there, whatever the Side size. We chose one Leaderboard per Ladder to keep the app simple and fun, since friends mix 1v1 and 2v2 freely. The known cost is that a weaker Participant who often partners a stronger one can climb on their partner's skill. If that becomes a problem, the remedy is a smaller rating change for team Matches, not separate Ratings.

## Considered Options

- **Separate singles and doubles Ratings on one Ladder.** Two Leaderboards per Ladder, roughly doubling the screens and queries.
- **Side size fixed per Ladder.** Cleaner Ratings, but friends would need two Ladders for the same group of people.
